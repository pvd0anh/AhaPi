This video, created by *Thảo Lê*, serves as a practical tutorial for understanding and calculating **ratios**. A ratio compares two quantities, which can be expressed mathematically as $a:b$ or as the fraction $\frac{a}{b}$. The tutorial covers three illustrative examples:

* **Monkeys and Bananas (0:04-0:55):** By counting the items, the video establishes a ratio of 4 monkeys to 5 bananas. This is expressed as the ratio $4:5$ or the fraction $\frac{4}{5}$.
* **Rock Collection (0:56-2:16):** *Donna’s* collection contains various rocks: 6 pieces of granite, 3 pieces of marble, and 14 pieces of sandstone. The problem asks for the ratio of sandstone to marble, resulting in $14:3$ or $\frac{14}{3}$.
* **Dinner Time (2:16-3:37):** This final example looks at *Daphne’s* dinner, where there are 12 pieces in a bowl and 7 pieces in her hair. The ratio of bowl pieces to hair pieces is identified as $12:7$ or $\frac{12}{7}$.

[[Câu]] In the first example (0:04 - 0:55), there are 4 monkeys and 5 bananas. If you were to add 4 more monkeys and 5 more bananas, what would be the new ratio of monkeys to bananas?
[[Đáp án]] 8:10. The ratio remains equivalent, as you are doubling both quantities.

[[Câu]] Regarding the final example (2:16 - 3:37), Daphne has 12 pieces of food in her bowl and 7 pieces in her hair. If she eats 2 pieces from the bowl, what is the new ratio of food in the bowl to food in her hair?
[[Đáp án]] 10:7. By subtracting 2 from the 12 pieces in the bowl, you are left with 10 pieces, while the amount in her hair remains 7.
