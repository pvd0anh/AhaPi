## Equivalent Ratios

Two ratios are **equivalent** when they describe the same relationship, even though the numbers differ. You move between them by multiplying or dividing both parts by the same number.

$$2:3 \;=\; 4:6 \;=\; 6:9 \;=\; 20:30$$

### Simplest form

A ratio is in **simplest form** when the two parts share no common factor other than 1. To get there, divide both parts by their highest common factor.

$$24:36 \;\xrightarrow{\;\div 12\;}\; 2:3$$

> **Quick test for equivalence:** cross multiply. The ratios $a:b$ and $c:d$ are equivalent exactly when $a \times d = b \times c$.

Checking whether $4:6$ and $10:15$ are equivalent:

$$4 \times 15 = 60 \qquad 6 \times 10 = 60$$

The products match, so the ratios are equivalent.

## Ratios With Units

Before comparing, both quantities must be in the **same unit**. Comparing 50 cm to 2 m directly gives the wrong answer.

$$50\ \text{cm} : 2\ \text{m} \;=\; 50:200 \;=\; 1:4$$

| Written as | Convert to | Simplify |
|---|---|---|
| 30 min : 2 hours | $30:120$ | $1:4$ |
| 200 g : 1 kg | $200:1000$ | $1:5$ |

[[EXERCISES]]

[[Q]] Write $45:60$ in its simplest form.
[[A]] The highest common factor is 15, so $45:60=3:4$.

[[Q]] Are $9:12$ and $15:20$ equivalent? Show your working with cross multiplication.
[[A]] $9\times 20=180$ and $12\times 15=180$. The products are equal, so yes, they are equivalent — both simplify to $3:4$.

[[Q]] Simplify the ratio 40 minutes to 1 hour.
[[A]] Convert to the same unit first: 1 hour is 60 minutes, so the ratio is $40:60$, which simplifies to $2:3$.
