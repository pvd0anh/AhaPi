## Carrying Across the Decimal Point

A second addition example, this time where the tenths column overflows.

**Worked example.** $27.85 + 9.47$

$$\begin{array}{r} 27.85 \\ +\ 9.47 \\ \hline 37.32 \end{array}$$

Working right to left:

1. Hundredths: $5+7=12$. Write $2$, carry $1$.
2. Tenths: $8+4+1=13$. Write $3$, carry $1$.
3. Ones: $7+9+1=17$. Write $7$, carry $1$.
4. Tens: $2+1=3$.

The decimal point in the answer sits directly below the points above it. A carry crosses the decimal point exactly like any other column — the point does not block it.

> **Estimate before you calculate.** $27.85$ is nearly $28$ and $9.47$ is nearly $9.5$, so the answer should be close to $37.5$. Getting $3.732$ or $373.2$ would immediately look wrong.

## Adding Numbers of Different Lengths

$$6 + 0.375 + 12.9 \;=\; 6.000 + 0.375 + 12.900 \;=\; 19.275$$

A whole number has an invisible decimal point after it: $6 = 6.000$.
