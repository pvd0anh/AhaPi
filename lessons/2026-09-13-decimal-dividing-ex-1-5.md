## Dividing Decimals

Division is the one operation where you do **not** simply line up the points. Instead you first turn the divisor into a whole number.

$$\text{Rule: multiply both numbers by the same power of } 10 \text{ until the divisor is whole.}$$

This works because a division is a fraction, and scaling top and bottom by the same amount leaves the answer unchanged:

$$\frac{4.8}{0.6}=\frac{4.8 \times 10}{0.6 \times 10}=\frac{48}{6}=8$$

**Worked example.** $7.36 \div 0.4$

Multiply both by 10: $73.6 \div 4$. Now divide as usual, keeping the decimal point in the answer directly above the point in $73.6$:

$$73.6 \div 4 = 18.4$$

### Dividing by a whole number

If the divisor is already whole, divide straight away and keep the point aligned:

$$9.45 \div 5 = 1.89$$

> **Sanity check:** dividing by a number smaller than 1 makes the answer **bigger** than what you started with. $7.36 \div 0.4 = 18.4$ is larger than $7.36$, and that is correct, not a mistake.
